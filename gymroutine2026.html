Recomp Protocol v2

Return to Form

4-day Upper/Lower · 60-min lunch sessions · Aesthetics priority · Start June 15, 2026

Phase Roadmap
80kg · 24% BF → 71kg · 12% BF · ~24 weeks
Phase 1 — Neural Reactivation
Jun 15 – Jul 12 · Weeks 1–4
Rebuild motor patterns, joint conditioning, moderate loads. RPE 6–7.
~78kg · ~22% BF
Phase 2 — Progressive Overload
Jul 13 – Sep 6 · Weeks 5–12
Add weight weekly on compounds. Drop sets on isolation. This is where muscle memory pays off.
~76kg · ~17–19% BF
Phase 3 — Cut
Sep 7 – Nov 1 · Weeks 13–20
400–500 kcal deficit. Preserve muscle, strip fat. Tighter tracking required. One high-carb refeed day per week at maintenance to manage leptin and adherence.
~72kg · ~13–14% BF
Phase 4 — Deep Cut (Optional)
Nov 2 – Nov 29 · Weeks 21–24
Push to 12% if desired. Harder, slower, requires discipline.
~71kg · ~12% BF

Meal Prep System
Make the food side effortless so you can focus on training
01Sunday meal prep: batch cook 2 post-workout options for the week (e.g., teriyaki chicken + turkey burrito bowl). Split into 5 containers.
02Pre-workout meals should take <5 min to assemble. Keep wraps, rice cakes, tuna, eggs, and yoghurt stocked.
03Dinners are cooked fresh since you're eating with your partner. Most take 25–35 min.
04Buy sauces in bulk: soy sauce, sriracha, sesame oil, tahini, chipotle in adobo, fish sauce. These rotate across everything.
Rest Day Options
Pick 1–2 per rest day. Recovery is where growth happens.
01Light 20–30 min walk or cycle (active recovery, LISS)
02Mobility work / foam rolling — hips, thoracic spine, shoulders
03Heavy bag — 20 min max, don't turn it into HIIT during recomp
04GTG pull-ups on garden bar — 50% of max reps, spread through the day
REMEMBER — Recomp works because you're a detrained lifter with muscle memory and elevated body fat. Eat at maintenance, hit 160g+ protein daily, train 4×/week, sleep 7–8 hrs. After week 12 reassess: if above 18% BF, transition to a cut with one refeed day per week at maintenance calories (extra carbs, not fat). If already seeing definition, keep recomping.
